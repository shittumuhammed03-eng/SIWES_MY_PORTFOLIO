Project Overview

A Complete Digital School Experience for Administrators, Teachers, Students & Parents

Maverique SchoolOS is a comprehensive school management platform designed to streamline property rental transactions by tenants, caretakers, and independent landlords on a single unified platform. As the sole engineer, I designed, architected, and engineered the entire platform from concept to deployment.

The platform addresses key challenges faced by schools, including the high cost of website development, reliance on physical paperwork, disorganized fee management, and limited access to digital learning tools. By bringing a branded school website, role-based portals, computer-based testing (CBT), fee management, and an installable school app into one free platform, maverique SchoolOS simplifies school administration and improves communication between schools, staff, students, and parents. This reduces administrative workload, improves access to school services, and provides schools with a centralized digital system without the expense and complexity of building and maintaining multiple separate solutions.

Platform Capabilities

Role-Based Portals for Every School Community

Role-Based Portals Secure School Access User Profiles

Implemented a comprehensive authentication and access-control system for Maverique SchoolOS with distinct user roles for administrators, teachers, students, and parents. Secure authentication ensures that users can only access features and information relevant to their roles, while role-based dashboards provide each user type with a tailored experience for managing school activities, learning, communication, and administrative tasks.

Free Branded School Website

Built a dynamic, fully branded school website that allows schools to showcase their identity, share important information, and provide students, parents, and staff with easy access to school resources. Product Vision & SchoolOS Execution

From School Administration Problems to One Connected Platform

As the sole engineer on Maverique SchoolOS, I took full ownership of the product lifecycle, from ideation and planning through development and deployment. This included:

Product Discovery:Defined the product vision, MVP scope, and key user roles for schools, administrators, teachers, students, and parents. Identified common challenges such as expensive school website development, manual record-keeping, fragmented fee management, and limited access to digital assessment tools.

Roadmap Planning: Developed a phased product roadmap prioritizing four core MVP features: a free branded school website, role-based portals, school fees and payment management, and built-in CBT and digital assessments. Each feature was carefully scoped to provide practical value while keeping the platform simple and accessible for schools.

Solo Execution: Single-handedly designed the system architecture, developed the full-stack application, implemented the core MVP features, and managed the deployment infrastructure. As the sole engineer, I was responsible for turning the product vision into a functional, scalable platform for schools.
